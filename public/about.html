About CareerWorth

CareerWorth is a browser-based career and compensation planner. It helps people compare job offers and explore how salary, bonuses, equity, living costs, estimated taxes, retirement contributions and investment assumptions may affect their projected savings over time.

How it works

Enter your own assumptions and adjust them to explore different scenarios. Calculations run in your browser, and your plan is saved locally on your device. Share links and backups may include sensitive financial inputs, so share them only with people you trust. Read the privacy notice for details, including optional analytics.

Scope and limitations

Results are estimates based on the assumptions and tax rules described in the planner. They are not a tax return, a guarantee of future performance, or personalized financial, tax or legal advice. Consult original source documents and qualified professionals when making decisions. The product guide explains the model, its limits and agent use.
