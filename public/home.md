# CareerWorth: job offer comparison and compensation planner

CareerWorth is a browser-based planner for comparing job offers, compensation, living costs, estimated taxes, equity and projected savings. Explore salary and bonuses, RSU vesting, retirement contributions, employer matches and investment growth under your own assumptions.

Calculations run in your browser and plans are stored locally. Share links and backups can contain sensitive financial inputs; share them only with people you trust. Results are estimates, not personalized financial, tax or legal advice.
