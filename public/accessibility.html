Accessibility

Last updated November 9, 2026

CareerWorth aims to make the planner usable with keyboards, screen readers, magnification, and different input methods. We use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a development target. This statement is not a claim of certified or complete conformance; a full independent audit across assistive technologies and applicable jurisdictions has not been completed.

Current review

The site provides visible keyboard focus, a skip link, labeled planner controls, semantic headings and tables, and text alternatives for the projection chart. Some interactive results and dense planning tables may still need additional assistive-technology review.

This app does not currently publish contact details. For an accessibility barrier, contact the site operator or hosting service that provided access to CareerWorth. Include the page, the task you were trying to complete, and your browser or assistive technology if you are comfortable sharing it; please do not send private financial inputs.

Applicable requirements depend on the service

Accessibility laws depend on the operator, audience, service, and location. For example, the U.S. ADA applies to state and local governments and businesses open to the public; Section 508 applies to covered U.S. federal agency information and communication technology; and the European Accessibility Act covers specified products and services, with exclusions including microenterprises providing services. This general statement does not determine whether a specific law applies to a particular deployment.
