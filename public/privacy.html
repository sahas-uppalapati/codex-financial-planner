CareerWorth planner

Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026

CareerWorth is a career and compensation planning tool. This page describes the browser storage, sharing, backups, and optional analytics in this version of the site.

Planner information and browser storage

Your planner inputs and saved plan are stored in this browser using local storage. The selected view mode is stored in session storage. The app does not create an account or send planner values to an application server for calculation. Your browser or device provider may still back up or sync browser data according to your own settings. Clearing site data removes the locally saved plan.

Share links and backups

Financial inputs are not automatically placed in the address bar. Share links are created explicitly and include both offers and startup details; anyone with a link can read them. Incoming links require confirmation before replacing your plan. Backups include the complete plan and startup details and are stored wherever you save them. Keep links and backups private.

Visit and optional usage analytics

If analytics are configured for a deployment, the planner sends a basic page view to PostHog when it opens. Optional usage analytics are off unless you choose “Allow usage analytics” in the footer. With that choice, the app also records comparison additions, CSV exports, and consent. Your choice is stored in local storage and you can turn usage analytics off in the footer. Analytics events do not intentionally include salary, balances, tax values, company names, scenario contents, or share-link values; query strings and fragments are removed from URL properties before sending. Automatic event capture, exception capture, session recording, surveys, feature flags, and person profiles are disabled. PostHog uses in-memory persistence and IP processing is disabled. PostHog and the hosting provider may still process technical request information, and the host may keep operational logs; processing and retention depend on provider terms and deployment configuration.

Hosting and external sources

The site host necessarily receives network requests to serve the app and may keep operational logs under its own configuration. The planner links to third-party sources; when you follow those links, those providers handle your visit under their own privacy terms.

Children and changes

This planner is intended for adults making career and financial plans, not for children. This notice may be updated when the site’s behavior changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
