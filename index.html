CareerWorth: compare job offers and plan your savings

CareerWorth is a browser-based job offer comparison and compensation planner. Compare salary, bonuses, restricted stock units, retirement benefits and living costs to understand how different career choices could affect your future savings.

Explore your assumptions

Model estimated federal, state, local and payroll taxes in supported locations, or enter an effective tax rate. Explore equity vesting, retirement contributions, employer matches, investment growth and changes in expenses over time. Results are estimates and depend on the assumptions you provide.

Privacy and limitations

Calculations run in your browser and your plan is stored locally. Share links and backups can contain financial information and should be shared only with people you trust. CareerWorth is a planning tool, not personalized financial, tax or legal advice. Enable JavaScript to use the interactive planner.
